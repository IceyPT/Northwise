About this site

The compass has two axes. The economic axis runs from left (more public ownership and redistribution) to right (more free-market and low taxes). The social axis runs from libertarian (more personal freedom) to authoritarian (more state authority, order and tradition).

This is a short, simplified quiz written for fun. It is not affiliated with the Political Compass organisation, and the wording of any questions shapes the result, so treat it as a conversation starter rather than a measurement.

Scores run from -10 to +10 on each axis. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
